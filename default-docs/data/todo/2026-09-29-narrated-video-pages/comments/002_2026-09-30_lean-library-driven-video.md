Clarified by sidhantha on 2026-09-30: no MP4 or any rendered video file, cached or stored. A video artifact stays lean (the authored file under about 10 KB) and fast: elements and objects moving, images allowed, driven by a rich library of components (frames, icons, charts, layouts) and a grid, quadrant and location system for placement. PowerPoint-level motion, not film-level. It needs a good voiceover, high-quality animation and frames, and a strong skill for authoring these videos.
