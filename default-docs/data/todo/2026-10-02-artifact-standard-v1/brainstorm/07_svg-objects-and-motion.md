## SVG objects and proper explanatory motion

The owner asked for motion graphics that explain concepts using SVGs and vector objects, kept simpler than complex film animation.

Requested objects and analogy material include:

- Computers and servers.
- Birds.
- Books.
- Accounts and table views.
- Humans.
- Trees.

Motion should communicate a concept: a request traveling from a computer to a server, a book opening to reveal knowledge, a selected account revealing a table, a bird illustrating movement or transfer, or a tree illustrating growth or hierarchy.

Reusable vector objects can expose named parts, pivots and meaningful poses. An actor should be usable in several scenes and visual families rather than being a single hardcoded animation.

**Implementation considerations:** define transforms, easing, transitions, bounded loops and state changes; keep object identity stable; specify seeking/replay behavior and readable reduced-motion states. A reusable motion component should coordinate with interaction and narration without depending on a hidden engine-only sequence.

The user requested useful concept motion, not complex cinema production. The scope can grow through concrete examples rather than an unrestricted general animation studio.
