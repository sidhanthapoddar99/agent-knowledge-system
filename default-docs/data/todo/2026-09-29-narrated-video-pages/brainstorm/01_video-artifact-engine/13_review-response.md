title: "Review response"
---

**An independent critic reviewed this design on 2026-10-01 and judged it sound with fixes. All five major findings are fixed. Of the nine minor ones, eight are taken as suggested and one partly.** The one not taken as written is renaming the `scripts` category. The critic also offered to ask sidhantha what "frames" means; the design covers both meanings instead, so no question is needed. Each row names the file that now carries the fix.

## Major findings

| # | Finding | What changed | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | misaki-rs compiles espeak-ng (GPL-3.0) by default; without it, unknown words such as "agentks" are spelled letter by letter, and v1 had no fix | Confirmed on crates.io: misaki-rs 0.6.0 is MIT (not Apache 2.0 as first written), and its default `espeak` feature pulls in `espeak-rs`. The helper builds it with `default-features = false`. A pronunciation list (`config/video.yaml` and a per-video `pronounce:`) is in v1. The helper's `g2p` request lists unknown words, `video.unknown-word` is an error, and the helper refuses to speak a beat with one. The GPL helper road is recorded and put to sidhantha as a question. The fallback candidates are corrected: Kitten TTS and sherpa-onnx's text path also use espeak-ng | [07 §03, §05, §13](./07_voiceover.md), [03 §03, §06](./03_artifact-format.md), [index questions 2 and 4](./01_index.md#questions-for-sidhantha) |
| 2 | `video.text-fit` was a compiler warning, but only the browser can measure text | The player owns layout, text fit and overlap, and reports `layout.text-fit`, `layout.overflow` and `layout.overlap`. Rust does no text measurement: styles use the site's font stacks, so Rust cannot know the font, and a guess would be a wrong answer that looks right. The player waits for `document.fonts.ready` before laying out | [03 §06 layer 3](./03_artifact-format.md#layer-3-the-players-layout-diagnostics), [05 §03](./05_layout-system.md), [06 §07](./06_player.md#07-layout-diagnostics-and-the-review-sheet) |
| 3 | The skill had no visual verification | The standalone page gains a review sheet (`?sheet`): every slide's end state and each morph's midpoint on one screen, with the diagnostics. The skill's step 9 is a look gate in light and dark with the Playwright tools, and the never-table forbids calling a video done without it. The evaluation keeps the screenshots | [06 §07](./06_player.md#07-layout-diagnostics-and-the-review-sheet), [10 §02, §04](./10_authoring-skill.md) |
| 4 | Inlining third-party SVG behind a denylist is unsafe, and ids clash | The compiler parses each SVG and writes back only an allowlist of elements, attributes and same-file references; everything else is an error. `<style>` and SMIL elements are refused. Ids are prefixed per use. The library system note records this as the one sanctioned exception to sandboxing, with the reason | [08 SVG safety](./08_library-components.md#svg-safety-an-allowlist), [09 §06](./09_architecture-fit.md), [11](./11_changes-to-existing-design.md) |
| 5 | 74 icons, and no illustrations or images, is not "tons of options" | The full Lucide set (1,857 icons, ISC, checked on npm) is imported with its tags. Day one adds 12 illustrations. Library images stay at zero in v1, stated plainly: images come from the project's own `assets/` | [08 §08](./08_library-components.md#08-the-day-one-set) |

## Minor findings

| # | Finding | What changed | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | `@word` prefix matching lands on the wrong word silently | Whole words only, ignoring case and punctuation, with `#n` for repeats; no match is an error. All 42 anchors in the example were re-checked as whole words | [04 §03](./04_scenes-and-timeline.md#03-actions) |
| 7 | Two alternating audio elements add gaps, iOS unlock trouble and Safari risk | The clip stays the unit of generation. Delivery becomes one Ogg Opus stream per video, joined by copying packets, with silence packets filling pauses, so audio time equals video time. The voice spike tests it in four browsers, with per-beat clips and a WebM copy as fallbacks | [07 §07](./07_voiceover.md#07-one-stream-per-video), [06 §05](./06_player.md#05-the-clock) |
| 8 | Audio stored per project is regenerated for every clone and every CI deploy | Machine-wide, content-keyed store at `~/.agentks/audio/`. CI caches it, with the helper and model. `cache clean` keeps clips that scanned projects use. Export and import commands were not added: the CI cache covers the need, and the store stays simpler without them | [07 §08](./07_voiceover.md#08-the-store) |
| 9 | The standalone artifact waited for the app's page kind and islands | Split: T7a is the standalone artifact (one shell writer, `agentks video preview` with no server, the route), T7b the app pages. T7a comes first | [index build plan](./01_index.md#build-plan), [09](./09_architecture-fit.md) |
| 10 | The library restructure need not wait for the player | Split: T4a (folder move, `category`, the check rule, the Lucide import) runs in wave 1, before the first tag; T4b (video components) follows T1 | [index build plan](./01_index.md#build-plan) |
| 11 | Nothing enforced the 10 KB target, and the action count was wrong | `video.file-size` warns past 10 KB; the skill caps a video at about four minutes and splits longer topics into a series. The count is corrected to 45 actions, re-measured with the gzip size | [03 §05, §06](./03_artifact-format.md), [10](./10_authoring-skill.md) |
| 12 | Spring presets would feel stiff; `focus` may blur text | Presets may use CSS `linear()` easing. T1 measures text sharpness after `focus` and stage scaling, and picks `zoom` or a transform from that. `will-change` is set only while an animation runs | [04 §04](./04_scenes-and-timeline.md#04-presets), [05 §01](./05_layout-system.md), [index T1](./01_index.md#build-plan) |
| 13 | "Frames" may mean containers; no annotation category; `scripts` clashes with the tooling folder | Partly taken. Frames now cover device chrome and containers (card, callout, bubble …), since both are an SVG with a slot, so no question is needed. A new `annotations` category holds circles, underlines, brackets and arrows drawn by emphasis presets. **Not taken: the `scripts` rename.** sidhantha named that category himself ([migration comment 003](../../../2026-09-29-rust-core-engine-migration/comments/003_2026-09-30_library-components-layout.md)), and `components/scripts/` never meets the repository's top-level `scripts/` | [08 §03](./08_library-components.md#03-the-categories) |
| 14 | T3 needs a YAML parser with node positions, and that lived only here | Listed as a change to 030/30 now, naming `saphyr` and the `jsonschema` crate (versions and licences checked) | [11](./11_changes-to-existing-design.md), [12 §05](./12_research.md) |
